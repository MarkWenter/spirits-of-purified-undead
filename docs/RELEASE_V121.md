# 1.2.1 · 纯净触碰镶嵌修复 / Pure Touch socket fix

适用于 Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge 和 1.21.1 NeoForge。

修复《锻造之旅 1.4.4》等整合包中纯净触碰已经镶嵌成功，扩展剑却不显示「秽鬼守护」、挥剑也没有剑气的问题。新版直接读取 Tetra 已安装部件上的词条。更新后拿起原来的物品即可，无需重新镶嵌。

现在，任何能镶嵌纯净触碰的 Tetra 改造物品，包括镐、铲和切石刃，都可以在主手挥击时释放一道剑气。对空气挥击同样生效。剑气伤害取决于持物时的攻击伤害和本次攻击蓄力。取下宝石或物品损坏后，效果停止。

## English

For Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge and 1.21.1 NeoForge.

Fixed an issue in modpacks such as Journey of Forging 1.4.4 where Pure Touch could be socketed into an add-on sword, but Blighted Guardian would neither appear on the item nor release a wave. The mod now reads the effect from the installed Tetra modules. Existing sockets work immediately after updating.

Any Tetra modular item that accepts Pure Touch, including pickaxes, shovels and stonecutter blades, can now release a wave when swung in the main hand, even in the air. Wave damage scales with your attack damage while holding the item and your attack charge. Removing the gem or breaking the item disables the effect.
