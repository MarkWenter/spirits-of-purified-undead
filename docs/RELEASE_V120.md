# 涤净亡魂 1.2 · 费林更新 / Spirits of Purified Undead 1.2 · The Ferin Update

## 中文

**费林的五连斩从你身旁的光点起手。** 斜斩接回挑，再转入下劈、回旋和翻身终斩。每一段都有独立的飞行轨迹，暖白刃光后拖着紫焰，离手的剑气会短暂追踪目标。

- **骑士寂夜**是与车万女仆联动的新饰品。在女仆祭坛制成后，手持饰品蹲下使用以绑定，再放入自己女仆的饰品栏。费林出剑时，她的虚影会在光点处助战，她的攻击属性也会加成协同斩击。适配的酒狐模型可演出完整的五段剑术。
- **秽鬼之守护**获得了新图标、像素手持模型和绕刃旋转的半透明气旋。亲手挥剑时，费林的剑、西丽德的链锤、西丽亚的巨锤、格洛特的战锤和尤里乌斯的战枪有同等机会从握剑侧飞出，各自带着贴合刃口、锤面或枪尖的紫色光迹。
- **物品与装备重绘。** 材料、饰品、莉莉手记、八枚秽鬼追忆、纯净触碰和纯净禁果换上新的像素贴图。无垢盔甲同时更新了图标和穿戴外观，秽鬼刷怪蛋的暗红纹样也更清晰。纯化铸造器则有了凹入的炉腔、分层石框和悬剑。
- **四类 Curios 饰品槽**各有新的空槽剪影，可一眼区分古老民族之契、不死战士、白巫女遗物和流浪日志。白巫女手记补全了纯化铸造器等物品的配方，并收录骑士寂夜。佩戴古老民族之契时，手持怨念污秽右键可转化的生物，即可将其变为对应的秽鬼。
- **Tetra 与 Better Combat 联动。** 在 Tetra 加工台镶嵌纯净触碰消耗 1 点完整度，增加 512 点耐久和 2 点攻击伤害。近战武器获得「秽鬼守护」，剑气伤害取决于手中武器。启用 Better Combat 的按住攻击后，秽鬼之守护本体和镶嵌武器每次主手出招都会发出一道剑气。

## English

**Ferin’s five-hit combo now starts at the wisp beside you.** A diagonal cut leads into a rising return cut, an overhead strike, a spinning sweep, and a somersault finisher. Each slash flies along its own path, briefly tracking its target with a warm white edge and a trail of violet flame.

- **Knight Nocturne** is a new charm for Touhou Little Maid. Craft it at the maid altar, sneak and use it to bind it to yourself, then place it in your maid’s accessory slot. When Ferin strikes, her apparition joins the attack at the wisp, and her attack attributes strengthen the assist slash. Supported Wine Fox models can perform the full five-hit sword combo.
- **Blighted Guardian** has a new inventory icon and a pixel-art held model with a translucent vortex circling the blade. Swing it yourself to send one of five spectral weapons from your sword hand. Ferin’s sword, Siegrid’s flail, Silva’s greathammer, Groth’s warhammer, and Julius’s lance are equally likely to appear. Violet trails follow each weapon’s striking surface as it flies.
- **Redrawn items and equipment** include crafting materials, accessories, Lily’s Diary, all eight Blighted Memories, Pure Touch, and Pure Forbidden Fruit. Immaculate Armor has new inventory icons and worn textures, and Blighted spawn eggs have clearer crimson markings. The Purification Foundry now has a recessed chamber, a layered stone frame, and a suspended sword.
- **Four new Curios slot silhouettes** distinguish the Ancient Contract, Undead Warriors, White Witch Relics, and Wanderer Log. The White Witch’s Diary now shows missing recipes, including the Purification Foundry, and covers Knight Nocturne. While wearing the Ancient Contract, use a Blighted Spirit on an eligible mob to turn it into its Blighted counterpart.
- **Tetra and Better Combat integration.** Socketing Pure Touch at a Tetra workbench costs 1 integrity and adds 512 durability and 2 attack damage. Melee weapons gain the Blighted Guardian effect, firing a wave whose damage scales with the weapon in hand. With Better Combat’s hold-to-attack enabled, both the original Blighted Guardian and socketed melee weapons release one wave per main-hand swing.
